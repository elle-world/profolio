# Project Brief

## Goal

Build a personal portfolio website that supports my transition toward AI product manager roles.

The website itself should also function as a web coding portfolio work. It should show my ability to use AI tools to clarify product positioning, structure information, design a coherent experience, and ship a public-facing website.

## Target Audience

- AI product hiring managers
- Product team leads
- Recruiters
- Founders or teams looking for product managers with taste, empathy, and execution ability
- People who want to understand my career direction and personal perspective

## Core Message

I am a C-end product manager with industrial design and UI design experience. I bring design taste, user empathy, and real consumer product experience into AI-assisted product building.

## Why This Project Exists

I do not yet have formal AI product experience, so this project is designed to create credible evidence of my transition.

It will show:

- how I think about AI product opportunities
- how I understand user problems and experience details
- how I use AI tools to move from idea to prototype
- how I communicate product thinking through polished web experiences
- how my design background shapes my product judgment

## V1 Scope

- Personal introduction
- Career transition narrative
- Resume download
- AI product and web coding works
- Product brief pages or case sections
- Contact and social media links
- Minimal, tech-forward visual design

## Out Of Scope For V1

- Complex backend systems
- User login
- Full blog CMS
- Real financial advice features
- Production-grade AI API integration
- Overly decorative travel photography as the main visual identity

## Success Criteria

The first public version is successful if:

- a hiring manager understands my positioning within 10 seconds
- the website feels polished and credible
- my resume is easy to find and download
- at least 2 portfolio works are visible, even if they are early prototypes
- the design reflects taste, clarity, and restraint
- the project can keep evolving after launch
