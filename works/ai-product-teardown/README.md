# AI Product Teardown Library

## Concept

A structured library for analyzing AI products from a product manager's perspective.

## Why This Work

This work demonstrates AI product judgment without requiring a complex technical build.

It can show:

- understanding of AI product patterns
- ability to evaluate user experience
- sensitivity to failure cases
- structured product analysis
- clear communication
