# Product Brief - AI Product Teardown Library

## Problem

When transitioning into AI product roles, it is important to show product judgment, not only tool usage.

## Target Audience

- Hiring managers
- Product leads
- AI product teams
- Myself as a learning archive

## Teardown Template

For each AI product:

```text
Product
Target user
Core job to be done
Where AI appears in the user flow
Key UX strengths
Failure cases
Trust and safety concerns
What I would improve
What I learned
```

## First Milestone

Analyze 3 AI products and publish them as concise case cards on the portfolio website.
