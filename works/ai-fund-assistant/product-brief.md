# Product Brief - AI Fund Assistant

## Problem

Many ordinary users struggle to understand fund performance, risk, holdings, and market changes. Existing financial information is often technical, fragmented, or emotionally stressful.

## Target User

Retail fund users who want to understand what is happening to their fund in simple language.

## Opportunity

AI can help translate complex fund information into user-friendly explanations, while also making risk boundaries clear.

## Possible Core Scenario

User asks:

```text
Why did this fund drop recently?
```

The assistant responds with:

- plain-language explanation
- possible market factors
- fund-specific context
- risk reminder
- suggested next questions

## Product Principles

- Do not provide direct investment advice.
- Explain uncertainty clearly.
- Help users understand, not blindly act.
- Reduce anxiety through clarity.
- Keep financial risk boundaries visible.

## Prototype Ideas

- Fund explanation card
- Chat-like Q&A
- Risk-level summary
- Holding breakdown
- Suggested follow-up questions
