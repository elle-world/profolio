# Product Brief - AI Travel Planner

## Problem

Travel planning can be time-consuming because users need to compare destinations, routes, restaurants, budgets, timing, and personal preferences across many sources.

## Target User

Independent travelers who want a personalized plan but still want control over the final itinerary.

## Opportunity

AI can generate a first draft of the trip plan, explain tradeoffs, and let users edit based on their preferences.

## Product Principles

- The plan should be editable.
- Recommendations should explain why they fit the user.
- The system should ask only necessary questions.
- The output should avoid feeling generic.
- The experience should preserve the joy of travel planning.

## Prototype Ideas

- Preference intake form
- AI itinerary card
- Map-inspired layout
- Editable day plan
- Alternative suggestions
